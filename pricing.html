info and pricing

Session packages

Below are my photo session packages. If you would like more information about any of them, or you have a special request, send me a message on the booking page. I can't wait to work with you.

Mini Session

$85
30 minutes

Mini sessions are designed for a quick photo session to finally get those photos you have been putting off. Perfect for seniors, maternity, solo portraits, back to school photos, and more.

  • 10 to 20 edited images (I always over shoot)
  • Delivered within 2 to 3 weeks
  • Online gallery for easy downloading and sharing

Couples

$150
1 hour

A fun, special photo shoot devoted to capturing your love story, whether it is a new love, an engagement, or just because. Pick a location you love near Huntsville, or I can suggest some beautiful spots here in town.

  • 15 to 25 edited images (I always over shoot)
  • Delivered within 2 to 3 weeks
  • Online gallery for easy downloading and sharing

Family

$225
1 hour · inquire for longer sessions

A one hour session at your location of choice within a reasonable distance of Huntsville, Texas. A fun, easy, carefree hour dedicated to capturing your family life.

  • 30 to 50 edited images (I always over shoot)
  • Delivered within 2 to 3 weeks
  • Online gallery for easy downloading and sharing

Weddings

$900
Travel fees may apply

Designed especially for documenting your special day. From the moment I arrive at your venue, I am there to capture every moment, posed and candid. I can't wait to be a part of it.

  • 150 to 450 photos of your beautiful day
  • Delivered within 6 weeks
  • Online gallery for easy downloading and sharing

I am available to the Huntsville, TX community and surrounding areas. Farther away? Send me a message and we will talk it through. Travel fees may apply.
