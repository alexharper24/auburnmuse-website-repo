FAQ

Questions I hear a lot

Can we schedule a consultation or phone call before booking?

Of course. Send me a message above and I will get back to you as soon as I can.

Can I use the photos for social media and marketing materials?

Yes. Once you receive your online gallery, the photos are yours to do with what you wish.

Do you charge by the hour, or is there a flat rate for an event?

By the event. My standard sessions are approximately one hour, and you will receive countless photos with hours of labor of love poured in.

Are travel expenses included in your packages?

I am available to the Huntsville, TX community and surrounding areas. If you are outside Huntsville or would like me to travel to you, send me a message and we will discuss it.
